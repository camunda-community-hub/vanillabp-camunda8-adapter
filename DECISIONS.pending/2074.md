### A refusal because of a suspended workflow is reported to the extensions

Proposed by story 2074. The rule itself is the platform's decision in
`adapter-platform-integration/DECISIONS.pending/2074.md`: the one call to an extension while workflows
run is synchronous, isolated and has no outbox. This entry says what this adapter does with it.

- `Camunda8SuspendedWorkflow` reports through the core's `SuspendedWorkflowReporter` just before it
  refuses the call (`Phase.CALL`) or lets the outbox entry wait (`Phase.WAITING`). So all five places
  which find a suspension report it: the two phase-one checks and the three phase-two commands. The
  record carries the process instance key the adapter asked about, as the workflow id.
- The probe of a service task does not report. The check of phase one for the same call follows it, and
  one call would be reported twice.
- The worker of a synchronous task does not report. It gets a `409` from its own completion and does not
  ask why, because that would cost two searches per refused completion (`Camunda8CommandRetry`, and the
  addendum to decision 36 in `DECISIONS.pending/2070.md`).
- The lines 8.8 and 8.9 never ask about a suspension, so they never report one.
- A report which fails is logged, and the adapter throws what it would have thrown anyway.

**Tests.** `Camunda8SuspendedWorkflowTest` (line 8.10) holds one report per phase-one refusal, one per
phase-two attempt, none for an active workflow or the probe, and a failing report.

**Where it is cited.** javadoc of `Camunda8SuspendedWorkflow`, the wiki page `A-suspended-workflow`.
